# M4 Sampling arms

Extent: dev. The speed check on the full extent follows after full M2. Date: 2026-11-15.

## Problem at the top: acceptance check 3 (cluster uniformity) failed on attempt 1, passed on attempt 2

Rule: over 20,000 draws, every PSU's selection frequency vs pi must give |z| < 4. A0 (max |z| 2.04 over 48 tiles) and B2 (3.46 over 2,941 chips) pass. A1 gave max |z| = 4.26 over 2,695 complete chips.

Diagnosis (logs/M4_dev_A1_diagnosis.out): this is multiplicity, not a sampler fault. With 2,695 PSUs, the chance that at least one exceeds |z| = 4 under perfect sampling is about 2,695 x 6.3e-5 = 0.17. In three further 20,000-draw runs, within each tag stratum the z-scores have mean 0.000 and SD 0.88 to 1.05. The number with |z| > 3 was 10, 7 and 7, against 7.3 expected by chance, and the max |z| was 3.49, 3.69 and 3.59. The PI approved a Bonferroni limit over the PSUs tested in each arm, plus a per-stratum SD check on z (DECISIONS.md entry 24). Attempt 2, with the same seeds: A0 max |z| 2.04 vs limit 3.71; A1 4.26 vs 4.63; B2 3.46 vs 4.65. Every per-stratum SD of z is inside its chi-square band, for example A1 tag 2 (1,826 chips): 1.039, band 0.946 to 1.055.

## What was built

- `allocation.py`: minimum plus proportional or Neyman allocation, largest-remainder rounding, caps at N_h with redistribution. Every n_h >= 2 where N_h >= 2.
- `element.py`: SRS of cells (C) and stratified SRS (D, D2, F). Cached per-stratum index arrays (int32 order, memmap) in `data/<extent>/interim/strata_idx/`.
- `cluster.py`: SRS of tiles per state (A0); equal-per-tag chip selection with repeated equal redistribution of shortfalls (A1 over complete chips, B2 over all chips). Integer remainders go to tags in rarity order.
- `arms.py`: a `Context` that opens the memmaps, cached stratum indices and unit arrays once. `draw(arm, budget, rng)` draws a sample; `evaluate(draw)` returns the drawing arm and every arm that reuses the draw (A0 to A0w, A1 to B1 at tau 0, 0.75, 0.9 and 1.0, D to E). F draws one independent sample per model, and each sample feeds only that model's estimates. `actual_cvpr_test_split()` gives the fixed real split with pi = 20 / M_s, for documentation only. The real split was drawn from the CVPR frame, not ours. On dev it holds 4 tiles (1 MD, 3 DE), so pi there is capped at 1 and the numbers are not comparable to the full extent.
