# M3 Estimators and validation

Date: 2026-11-15. Synthetic, R and speed checks are extent-free; the real-data check uses the dev cell table.

## Problems at the top (both resolved with the PI)

1. Synthetic bias check, attempt 1 failed. 13 cluster UA_c cells exceeded 3 MCSE (bias about -0.001 at 40 PSUs) and one element cell reached -3.4 MCSE. The cluster cells match the analytic second-order ratio bias; the element cell was chance (independent seeds gave -0.70 and +0.86). The PI approved a theory-adjusted rule and a Bonferroni limit (DECISIONS.md entries 22 and 23). Attempt 2 passed.
2. Real-data A0w coverage failed: 0.901 to 0.908 at T = 5 (10 PSUs). The PI chose to record this as a finding and keep Wald intervals (entry 25). Expect A0w to under-cover at small T on the full extent too.

## What was built

- `params.py`: each ratio parameter as a pair of 6 x 5 coefficient matrices (A_y, A_x); `metrics_from_confusion` for every parameter, NaN on zero denominators.
- `weighted.py`: weighted confusion for element and cluster samples; per-cell indicators and per-PSU totals.
- `variance.py`: stratified element and stratified one-stage cluster linearization variance.
- `naive.py`: pooled confusion, binomial SE, McNemar z, chip-averaged mIoU.
- `bootstrap.py`: Rao-Wu rescaling bootstrap with the finite population correction (entry 20).
- `paired.py`: design-based OA difference (linearized); macroF1 and F1_c differences (bootstrap).
- `model_assisted.py`: post-stratified area estimator with its linearized SE.
- `estimate.py`: full estimate sets per design kind, and domain estimates.

These rare-class biases are ratio-estimator bias, not a code error. I compared the D biases with the analytic second-order bias, (R V(X) - Cov(Y, X)) / X^2, computed on the dev population under the D allocation at n = 1000 (logs/M3_dev_ratio_bias_check.out). Examples: WC barren PA observed +0.044, predicted +0.041; WC barren UA +0.070 vs +0.066; DW water PA +0.024 vs +0.026. Barren covers 0.29% and water 0.93% of dev cells. Their reference cells lie mostly in large strata, where a draw holds only a few of them, so the denominators are highly variable.

This matters for H1 condition 1, which requires rare-class UA, PA and area bias within max(2 MCSE, 0.005) for at least 95% of combinations. On dev, D at n = 1000 would fail that criterion. HYPOTHESES.md is not changed: dev results are never used to tune criteria. The full extent decides.
