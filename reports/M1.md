# M1 Data and grid

## Earth Engine gate (resolved)

The first dev pass ran without Earth Engine credentials. The PI authenticated on 2026-11-15 (project `hamed-gee`, recorded in `config/products.yaml`). Dynamic World was then downloaded for dev: 20 computePixels blocks of 1,024 x 1,024 cells, in 40 s. QA was rerun with all three products.

## What was built (dev extent)

- Boundaries: Census 2021 cartographic states (MD, DE), clipped to the dev bbox. The grid is EPSG:5070 with 10 m cells, origin snapped to multiples of 640 m (DECISIONS.md entry 21). It is 4,160 x 4,032 cells, upper left corner (1,703,680, 1,979,520). `state.tif` is rasterized by cell centre.
- Reference: Chesapeake LULC 2024 edition, 2021/22 county rasters for the four counties that intersect the dev bbox. These are Kent DE (10001), Caroline (24011), Queen Anne's (24035) and Talbot (24041). The release has no stand-alone land cover raster. The LULC classes are mapped to the 5 harmonized classes through the release's own crosswalk (`lc` column). The mapping is in `config/classes.yaml` under `lulc_to_harmonized`; see DECISIONS.md entries 15 to 19.
- Products: WorldCover 2021 v200 (2 tiles, N36W078 and N39W078), Esri/IO 2021 (`18S-2021`) and Dynamic World V1 (annual mode of `label` over 2021, Earth Engine, fetched on the exact grid). WC and IO are warped to the grid by nearest neighbour and crosswalked to 0..5.
- EPA Level III ecoregions: one ecoregion (63, Middle Atlantic Coastal Plain) covers the dev extent. Download URL: `https://dmap-prod-oms-edc.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/ORD/Ecoregions/us/us_eco_l3.zip`. It is linked from the EPA ecoregions page (checked 2026-11-15).
- CVPR spatial index: 232 MD and DE 2013 tiles. MD: 100 train, 5 val, 20 test. DE: 82 train, 5 val, 20 test.

### Coverage

All 11,540,200 in-state dev cells have ref_valid = 100. No cells have partial or no reference coverage. The dev bbox lies inland of the open bay, so this is expected. The full extent will test it properly.

### Quicklooks

I opened and checked windows 1 and 3. The 1 m reference and its 10 m plurality line up: roads, field edges and a solar array sit in the same place. WorldCover follows the same edges and shows the larger impervious features. It misses most narrow roads because they fall below 10 m plurality. Esri/IO is visibly smoother. Its built-up areas are rounded blobs larger than the true footprint, which explains its high impervious share (0.086 vs 0.034 in the reference). After the Dynamic World download I also checked window 2. DW is aligned with the others and, like IO, draws built-up areas as large smooth patches (impervious share 0.065 vs 0.034 in the reference). I saw no crosswalk errors: tree, low vegetation and impervious land in the expected places for all three products.

## Deviations and fixes

- Esri/IO STAC search with datetime 2021 also returned the 2020 item, whose end date is 2021-01-01. The first run used it, because it sorted first. Fixed by keeping only items whose start date is in 2021. The products step was rerun; the shift test and distributions above use the corrected map.
- Reference discovery uses one paged sciencebasepy query instead of one request per child item; the listing is cached in `data/reference_items.json`.
- Added helper modules not in the M0 layout: `data/io_utils.py`, `data/pipeline.py`, `population/pipeline.py`, `validation/`, `estimation/estimate.py`, `report/hypotheses.py`, `report/run.py`.
