## How different the models truly are (sets expectations for H3)

On dev, WorldCover is best on OA, with gaps of 0.034 to IO and 0.049 to DW. The IO vs DW gap is small: 0.015, below the 0.02 the H3 null clause refers to. On macroF1 the order changes: WC 0.657, DW 0.641, IO 0.602. The WC to DW gap is 0.017. The two metrics rank the models differently, and two of the three pairwise gaps are under 0.02 on at least one metric. Expect ranking errors at small budgets for every design on these pairs. Purity filtering alone raises true OA by 0.059 to 0.067 (tau 1.0 vs all), before any sampling.

## How different the models truly are (expectations for H3)

WC is far ahead: an OA gap of 0.081 to DW and 0.087 to IO, and macroF1 gaps of 0.055 and 0.095. Any reasonable design should find WC best. The DW vs IO OA gap is only 0.006, well under the 0.02 in the H3 null clause, and its sign reverses by state: DW leads in MD by 0.011, IO leads in DE by 0.019. A design that over-weights Delaware, as A0 does by about 5.4 times, can rank IO above DW on OA. On macroF1 the DW vs IO gap is 0.041, large enough for most designs. Purity filtering at tau = 1.0 alone raises true OA by 0.099 to 0.108.
