# M6 Figures, tables and results

## Dev draft (smoke test, not evidence)

Date: 2026-11-15. The six figures, both tables and a RESULTS draft were generated from `results/dev/` by `bowpilot report --extent dev`. The draft is `reports/RESULTS_dev_draft.md`. Its verdicts only exercise the reporting code: with 50 replicates the MCSE of a coverage near 0.95 is about 0.03.

### What was built

- `report/figures.py`: Figures 1 to 6, PNG at 300 dpi and PDF in `results/figures/`, PNG copies in `reports/figures/`. Arm colours by group, with distinct markers per arm, and a star for the real CVPR split.
- `report/tables.py`: Table 1 (truth per model, with state columns), Table 2 (OA and macroF1 bias, RMSE, coverage by arm and budget), and supplementary CSVs, all in `results/<extent>/tables/`.
- `report/hypotheses.py`: a mechanical evaluation of each numbered condition in HYPOTHESES.md. Design-set conditions are applied to each design arm separately.
- `report/results_md.py`: the RESULTS draft. For the full extent it never overwrites a hand-edited `reports/RESULTS.md`.

### Visual check of the dev figures

I opened every PNG. Fixes made after the first render:

1. Figure 1 picked the typical replicate by OA alone, so macroF1 often had no CI, and one panel (D2, WC barren UA) had an undefined value. The typical replicate is now chosen among bootstrap-subset replicates with all panel parameters defined.
2. Figure 5 showed reference cells outside the population, because ref_class is filled wherever county data exists. It is now masked to state > 0 and ref_valid = 100. I also added space between rows.
3. Figure 6 had overlapping log-axis tick labels. Ticks are now explicit.

After the fixes: labels are readable, legends are complete, and truth lines and the CVPR stars are visible. On dev, A0 shows almost no OA bias, because the dev frame has 24 tiles in each state. The full extent has far fewer DE tiles than MD tiles, which is where the protocol expects A0 bias.

### Acceptance checks

To be run on the full extent: figures in both formats, tables regenerated from `results/` alone, verdicts traceable to the summaries, and the adversarial review.

## Visual check (full)

I opened all six PNGs. Two fixes, then a re-render:
1. Figure 2: every arm selects WC in every replicate, so the left panel's y-axis autoscaled to 0.96 to 1.04. Fixed to 0 to 1.03.
2. Figure 5: the full extent is wide, which left a large blank band between rows. The figure height now follows the extent's aspect ratio.

After the fixes: labels are readable, legends are complete, and truth lines and CVPR stars are visible. Figure 4 uses DW, the model with the largest A1(0.9) OA bias.
